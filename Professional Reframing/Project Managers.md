### 🧠 Persona 5: Project Managers (PMs)

| Scenario | Comeback | Professional Reframe |
|----------|----------|------------------------|
| Plan go-lives without consulting security | "Deploying blindfolded doesn’t make it bold—it makes it risky." | "Security sign-off ensures smoother go-lives and fewer post-deployment issues." |
| Push MVPs that lack basic security controls | "Minimum viable product shouldn’t mean maximum liability." | "Let’s include baseline controls so we don’t compromise on customer trust." |
| Say security isn’t part of the scope | "Threat actors don’t check scope documents." | "Let’s update the scope to reflect today’s risk landscape." |
| Don’t account for privacy impact assessments | "User data isn’t just a checkbox—it’s a lawsuit if mishandled." | "We’ll embed privacy impact reviews early to avoid costly surprises later." |
| Fail to include rollback plans for risky changes | "No parachute? That’s not agile—it’s reckless." | "Rollback strategies are part of delivery resilience—let’s add it to the plan." |
| Book CABs without cyber participation | "Security isn’t optional seating—it’s a standing invite." | "Let’s ensure cyber SMEs are invited early in the change lifecycle." |
| Ignore risk register entries during sprints | "Ignoring the risk log won’t stop the incident log." | "We’ll integrate the risk register into sprint planning for ongoing visibility." |
| Skip pen testing for 'low-risk' apps | "Low risk until one login leaks everything." | "Even low-risk apps need minimum security validation pre-launch." |
| Rush timelines by dropping security reviews | "Speed without scrutiny = tech debt + breach risk." | "We can streamline reviews without skipping them—let’s define a lean process." |
| Don’t update Jira with cyber tickets | "Out of sight, out of compliance." | "Let’s integrate cyber tickets into your backlog to maintain visibility." |
| Blame delays on risk reviews | "The review didn’t delay the project—the risk did." | "Let’s time-box the reviews and collaborate early to avoid slowdowns." |
| Forget to onboard cyber SMEs in project kickoffs | "Security isn’t a patch—it’s a partner." | "We’ll assign a cyber SME to the kickoff checklist moving forward." |
| Don’t document platform-specific security needs | "If it’s not documented, it won’t be defended." | "We’ll define control requirements per platform to avoid rework." |
| Miss internal security sign-off for third-party vendors | "You’re trusting a stranger with the keys—without checking ID." | "All vendor launches need security review to protect our ecosystem." |
| Leave integrations with hardcoded tokens | "That’s not integration—it’s exploitation-ready." | "We’ll replace hardcoding with secure token management protocols." |
| Mix test and prod data without redaction | "That’s not testing—that’s exposing PII." | "We need obfuscation or synthetic data in non-prod environments." |
| Prioritize features over governance | "A flashy product with no guardrails is a breach waiting for a demo." | "Governance ensures scale, trust, and compliance—let’s balance the two." |
| Mislabel security tasks as 'nice-to-have' | "Until a breach forces them to be must-haves." | "Security tasks support product integrity—let’s tag them appropriately in the backlog." |
| Refuse user training plans | "Untrained users make for trained attackers." | "Let’s schedule security onboarding into delivery timelines." |
| Ask to launch before fixing known bugs | "So we’ll launch with vulnerabilities and just hope they don’t get found?" | "Let’s fix high-priority bugs before go-live to avoid customer impact and reputational risk." |
