### 🧠 Persona 4: Service Delivery Managers (SDMs)

| Scenario | Comeback | Professional Reframe |
|----------|----------|------------------------|
| Overpromise SLAs with no security oversight | "You can’t guarantee uptime on an unsecured door." | "Let’s factor cyber controls into SLA definitions for credible delivery." |
| Escalate only after breach or outage | "Reactive escalations are like calling the fire brigade after the ashes settle." | "Let’s establish proactive escalation paths for better response posture." |
| Claim security is not in their scope | "Neither is litigation—but here we are." | "Security touches delivery—let’s clarify roles and embed the right checkpoints." |
| Don’t enforce vendor security checks | "If the vendor’s house is on fire, ours might be next." | "We need assurance that vendor practices align with our security standards." |
| Delay onboarding of critical security tooling | "The longer we wait, the longer we’re exposed." | "Let’s align delivery timelines to priority risk mitigations." |
| Skip acceptance criteria for cyber controls | "Then don’t accept the risk either." | "Cyber acceptance criteria should be non-negotiable for production readiness." |
| Bypass CAB for urgent requests | "Urgency doesn’t excuse chaos." | "Fast-track requests still need structured visibility—let’s streamline, not skip." |
| Avoid incident response training | "If you can’t rehearse it, you can’t recover from it." | "We’ll include delivery leads in IR drills for end-to-end preparedness." |
| Don’t track device compliance across fleets | "If we can’t count them, we can’t protect them." | "Let’s integrate compliance dashboards into delivery tracking." |
| Delay device onboarding to MDM | "An unmanaged device is a rogue node." | "Timely onboarding ensures consistent control enforcement." |
| Fail to coordinate with SOC | "If you don't talk to the guards, don’t be shocked by the breach." | "Let’s formalize SOC coordination touchpoints in your process flows." |
| Don’t flag service abuse attempts | "Unflagged abuse becomes accepted behavior." | "Escalating misuse early reduces downstream risk." |
| Misreport security compliance to clients | "Overstating compliance is a liability, not a win." | "Let’s align messaging with verified posture to build long-term trust." |
| Don’t escalate recurring endpoint issues | "That’s not an endpoint—it’s a ticking bomb." | "Escalation should follow a pattern threshold—let’s define it together." |
| Agree to third-party access with no NDA or logging | "You just gave out the keys and turned off the cameras." | "All third-party access must be governed—let’s document and log properly." |
| Ignore audit timelines | "Auditors don’t accept ‘I forgot.’" | "We’ll help track audit milestones as part of service reviews." |
| Misunderstand who owns the security function | "When everyone's responsible, no one is." | "Let’s define ownership by domain—shared outcomes require clarity." |
| Avoid root cause analysis | "If you won’t find the root, the weeds will grow back." | "Let’s include RCA reviews as standard for high-impact issues." |
| Don’t track privileged access usage | "Privileged access without oversight is just a breach waiting for its moment." | "We’ll map privileged access to delivery ops and enforce proper controls." |
| Share creds between operators | "Shared creds mean shared blame—without traceability." | "We need individual accounts for accountability and compliance." |
