### 🧠 Persona 2: Executives (Board, CEO, CIO, CFO)

| Scenario | Comeback | Professional Reframe |
|----------|----------|------------------------|
| Asks for a cyber update in 30 seconds | "Sure—30 seconds now or 3 days of headlines later?" | "Here’s a high-level summary and a follow-up briefing request." |
| Dismisses threat intel because it didn’t happen to them | "That’s what the last breached CEO said too." | "Emerging threats should be assessed before they become actual incidents." |
| Asks to bypass SOC alerts for their VIP account | "Attackers target VIPs *because* they’re VIPs." | "We’ll tailor alerts but can’t remove safeguards—protection includes visibility." |
| Doesn’t attend cyber briefings | "We can’t secure what the top doesn’t see." | "A short monthly sync can keep you informed without disruption." |
| Says security is the CISO’s job, not theirs | "It’s everyone’s job when reputations are at stake." | "Leadership sets the tone—cyber culture begins at the top." |
| Shares sensitive data via WhatsApp | "Convenience shouldn't come at the cost of compliance." | "Let’s align on approved secure channels—there’s risk in shadow comms." |
| Says \"We trust our staff\" as a reason not to enforce controls | "Attackers trust them too—that’s the problem." | "Controls build trust by protecting everyone—not undermining it." |
| Reacts only after breaches, not before | "Let’s not make lessons learned a recurring calendar invite." | "Proactive investment prevents reactive cleanups." |
| Declines budget for critical detection tech | "If you think prevention is expensive, try recovery." | "I’ll map the tech to risk reduction outcomes for clearer ROI." |
| Blames CISO for vendor issues | "I’ll own security, not shortcuts." | "Let’s define clear roles across procurement, risk, and cyber moving forward." |
| Brings in a tech partner without consulting security | "Hope isn’t a strategy—and neither is surprise integration." | "Security due diligence should be built into the onboarding process." |
| Dismisses MFA due to \"inconvenience\" | "Inconvenient is better than compromised." | "MFA is now standard practice across all mature environments." |
| Confuses operational uptime with cyber maturity | "Uptime without protection is just a bigger target." | "Let’s differentiate availability from resiliency in reporting." |
| Doesn’t understand Zero Trust but uses the term | "Buzzwords don’t block breaches." | "I’m happy to explain how Zero Trust works in our architecture." |
| Pushes you to \"sell the message\" instead of fixing root cause | "Messaging can’t fix exposure—it just delays accountability." | "Let’s focus on resolving the core issue and align comms accordingly." |
| Forgets that risk tolerance must be informed, not emotional | "Emotions don’t negotiate with ransomware." | "Risk needs to be data-driven—we can pair gut feel with real metrics." |
| Challenges risk metrics they don’t like | "The data doesn’t change because it’s uncomfortable." | "We can unpack the metrics together—but the math stays the same." |
| Wants shorter policies without understanding implications | "Short doesn’t mean secure." | "I can shorten them—but the safeguards still need to be enforceable." |
| Asks for metrics but won't define strategic KPIs | "Directionless metrics waste time and dashboards." | "Let’s agree on KPIs that actually inform business outcomes." |
| Doesn’t know where company data is actually stored | "That’s how breaches go undetected—for months." | "We need data lineage to defend effectively. Let's map it together." |
